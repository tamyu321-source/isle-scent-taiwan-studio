# PURE WHITE recipe photographs

Three original concept photographs, generated once each with the built-in image generation tool. No variants or retries. Source PNGs are 1536 × 1024; the selected photographs were visually inspected and converted to WebP quality 88 without cropping, retouching or compositional changes. These are serving illustrations, not evidence of real products or exact weighed portions.

Saved assets:

- `public/images/pure-white-recipe-breakfast.webp`
- `public/images/pure-white-recipe-afternoon.webp`
- `public/images/pure-white-recipe-savory.webp`

Each actual prompt was the shared brief below, followed by its subject brief.

## Shared brief

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: standalone food photograph for a PURE WHITE recipe section, not a UI mockup.
Scene/backdrop: bright clean white tabletop, soft natural morning window light, coherent minimal editorial styling with cobalt-blue ceramic accents.
Style/medium: photorealistic clean editorial food photography, natural food colors, realistic edible textures and subtle natural shadows.
Output: one standalone landscape 3:2 photograph, approximately 1536 by 1024 pixels. No collage or panels.
Constraints: all ingredients clearly identifiable, realistic appropriate portions, thick white Greek yogurt; no text, logos, labels, packaging, watermarks, hands, or unrelated decorations.

## Breakfast

Primary request: breakfast blueberry banana oatmeal yogurt bowl.
Subject: a single cobalt-blue ceramic bowl holding 150 g of thick white Greek yogurt, topped with 20 g of instant oats, 50 g of sliced banana, and 40 g of fresh blueberries. Present a plausible single breakfast portion with all four ingredients visible and easily recognized. Keep a substantial visible area of creamy white yogurt, with gently formed spoon swirls.
Composition/framing: gentle top-down three-quarter angle, bowl fully visible with clean breathing room on the white tabletop, yogurt and oat textures clearly visible.
Avoid: any other fruit, nuts, seeds, honey, extra bowls, exaggerated ingredient quantities, text or UI elements.

## Afternoon

Primary request: afternoon honey orange almond yogurt glass.
Subject: one clear glass with visible layers of 150 g of thick white Greek yogurt and 80 g of peeled orange segments, a small 8 g honey drizzle and 10 g of sliced almonds on top. The glass sits on a cobalt-blue ceramic saucer. Keep the portions plausible for one snack, the orange segments distinctly recognizable and the white yogurt thick and creamy.
Composition/framing: eye-level with a slight downward viewing angle so both the transparent layered sides and top topping are visible; whole glass and saucer visible with clean breathing room.
Avoid: berries, oats, bananas, additional fruit, extra glasses, oversized overflowing portions, text or UI elements.

## Savory

Primary request: savory lemon parsley yogurt dip with vegetable sticks for four people to share.
Subject: a shallow white ceramic bowl with a cobalt-blue rim containing 300 g of thick white Greek yogurt dip. Lemon juice is mixed into the yogurt; a modest olive-oil swirl glistens on its surface, with finely chopped parsley and black pepper. Beside the bowl, place cucumber sticks and carrot sticks in realistic four-sharing portions. A small lemon wedge is permitted. The white yogurt remains the main subject and all vegetables and garnishes are easily identifiable.
Composition/framing: overhead three-quarter food spread, bowl and vegetable sticks fully visible on the bright white tabletop with clean breathing room.
Avoid: breakfast fruit, berries, oats, bananas, orange segments, honey, almonds, bread, crackers, excess oil, text or UI elements.

## Inspection notes

All three have appropriate food ingredients, natural yogurt texture and coherent white/cobalt styling. The breakfast oats appear rolled; instant processing and exact ingredient weights cannot be verified visually. The afternoon photo includes additional orange and ingredient prep bowls; the savory photo includes parsley sprigs and a serving platter. These props do not change the recipe. The UI labels the images as serving illustrations and provides the actual quantities separately.
